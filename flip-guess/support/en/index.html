Flip Guess Support

Try these steps to resolve common issues.

01

Decks will not download

  1. Check your internet connection.
  2. Close the app completely, then open it again.
  3. Pull down on the home screen to refresh.
02

An ad will not play

  1. Check your network connection.
  2. Wait a moment and try again.
  3. Ads may be temporarily unavailable depending on inventory in your region and at that time.
03

The tilt sensor is not working

  1. Hold your phone horizontally.
  2. Keep the starting position until the countdown finishes.
  3. Tilt the phone steadily and avoid shaking it too quickly.
04

I want to delete my data or account

Flip Guess does not offer accounts, so there is no account deletion request to make. Deleting the app also deletes its local settings, play history, and deck cache. Your operating system's backup and restore settings may affect this process.
