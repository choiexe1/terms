Flip GuessPrivacy Policy

Operator: Barisoft
Effective date: August 12, 2026

Summary

Flip Guess does not create user accounts or receive content entered or uploaded by users. However, third parties involved in deck synchronization and rewarded advertising, including Google AdMob, may process technical information.

1. Scope and general principles

This Policy applies to the Android and iOS versions of Flip Guess operated by Barisoft. The developer does not directly request information such as account details, names, email addresses, or phone numbers through the app, nor collect such information on its own servers. The app does not provide account creation or user content upload features. This does not mean that no information is processed at all: third-party services or network servers used by the app may process certain technical information as described below.

2. Information stored in the app

Game settings, play history, and downloaded deck caches are stored only on the user's device and are not transmitted to Barisoft servers. Deleting the app generally deletes this local data managed by the operating system. The exact behavior may vary depending on the operating system's backup and restore settings.

3. Device motion sensors

The app uses device motion sensors, such as the accelerometer, to recognize correct-answer and pass gestures. Sensor values are processed only on the device while determining game controls and are neither sent to Barisoft or third-party servers nor stored separately. The app does not use location, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions.

4. Deck synchronization and network requests

To keep deck lists and word data current, the app sends network requests for public JSON files hosted on GitHub Raw (raw.githubusercontent.com) and caches the results on the device. During these requests, GitHub or other server providers may process connection and technical information such as the IP address, request time, and user agent for service operation and security. Such processing is governed by the relevant provider's policies.

5. Google AdMob rewarded ads

The app uses rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. After a user watches an ad to completion, the selected deck becomes available for one round. AdMob and its advertising partners may process device identifiers such as advertising IDs, IP addresses, approximate location, device and app information, ad interactions such as impressions, clicks and viewing activity, and error or diagnostic information. This processing may be used to deliver ads, limit ad frequency, select personalized or non-personalized ads, prevent abuse, maintain security, and measure performance. The information actually processed may vary based on device settings, region, consent status, and Google's policies.

  • Downloading and updating public deck lists and words, and caching them on the device
  • Providing rewarded ads and confirming rewards
  • Preventing advertising abuse, maintaining security, diagnosing errors, and measuring performance

8. Retention and deletion

Barisoft does not operate a personal information database populated directly through the app. Game settings, play history, and deck caches are removed when the user deletes the app or clears its data in operating system settings. Retention periods and deletion methods for information processed by third-party services are governed by each provider's policies and legal obligations.

9. Security

Barisoft minimizes the information directly managed by the app and uses secure connections supported by the platform and service providers for deck and advertising communications. Nevertheless, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's privacy

The app does not ask children to create accounts or enter personal information, and Barisoft does not knowingly collect children's personal information directly. A parent or guardian who believes information relating to a child has been processed inappropriately may contact us at the email address below. Advertising processing may be subject to Google's applicable policies and the laws relevant to the user's region and age.

11. User choices

Users may choose whether to watch rewarded ads. Advertising ID and personalized advertising choices can be managed through the device's privacy or advertising settings. Users can delete local settings and play history by deleting the app. Because the app has no account feature, there is no separate account deletion request to submit.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may revise this Policy when the service or applicable laws change. Material changes will be posted on this page, and the effective date above will be updated.
